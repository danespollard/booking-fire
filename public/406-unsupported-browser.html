Booking Fire

Booking Fire needs a newer browser

Your browser is too old to run Booking Fire. Updating it takes a few minutes, and your calendar will be right where you left it.

Works with

  • iPhone and iPad on iOS 16.4 or newer
  • Safari 16.4 or newer on a Mac
  • Chrome, Edge or Firefox, kept up to date

Update an iPhone or iPad

Open Settings, tap General, then Software Update.

If your device can't update, try Booking Fire on a computer with an up-to-date browser.
